## 3. Two Approaches — Pick ONE for paper trading

### Option A: CAN SLIM Swing Trading (3-20 day holds)
**Based on:** O'Neil + Lynch + Livermore

**Screen:**
- EPS growth > 20% YoY (latest quarter)
- Sales growth accelerating
- New catalyst (product/management/industry change)
- Relative strength > 80 (top 20% of market)
- Industry group in top 20%
- NIFTY in uptrend or confirmed rally

**Entry:** Cup-with-handle breakout or flat base breakout on +50% volume

**Stop:** 7-8% below entry (O'Neil's rule)

**Target:** Let winners run; sell when story breaks

**When to skip:** Market in distribution, stock too extended (>10% above pivot)

### Option B: Intraday Momentum (same-day close)
**Based on:** Aziz + Murphy

**Screen:**
- Relative volume > 1.5 (unusual activity)
- Fundamental catalyst (earnings/news)
- Price moving independently of market

**Entry setups:**
- VWAP bounce (long near VWAP support)
- ABCD pattern (entry near C, target D)
- Bull flag (breakout above consolidation)

**Stop:** Below consolidation/VWAP/support

**Target:** 2:1 minimum reward; partial profits at 1:1

**When to skip:** Low relative volume, no catalyst, midday lull

## 5. Daily Routine

**Before market (8:30-9:00 AM):**
1. Check NIFTY 50 daily chart — trend?
2. Check FII/DII activity (if available)
3. Scan top gainers + unusual volume
4. Check earnings calendar for the day
5. Identify 2-3 candidates with setups
6. Write trade plan for each

**During market (9:15 AM-3:30 PM):**
1. Check pre-open sentiment (gap up/down)
2. Avoid first 15 min unless setup is clear
3. Execute only pre-planned trades
4. No chasing
5. Log every trade immediately after exit

**After market (4:00-4:30 PM):**
1. Close all positions (no overnight for intraday)
2. Journal all trades
3. Review P&L coldly — judge process, not money
4. Update watchlist for tomorrow
